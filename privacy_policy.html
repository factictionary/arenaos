Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 14, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to ArenaOS (“we”, “our”, or “us”). We provide a mobile and digital sports tournament management platform designed for housing societies, corporate campuses, clubs, and sports academies.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and related online services (collectively, the “Platform”). We are committed to transparency, data minimization, and protecting your personal privacy.

Core Promise: We do not sell your personal data. Your tournament registrations and community sports details are strictly used to organize matches, generate fixtures, record scores, and run live events.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in three ways: information you provide directly, information collected automatically, and tournament-related event records.

A. Information You Provide to Us

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number (optional), and authentication credentials provided via Google Sign-In or Firebase Authentication.
  • Player Profile & Passport: Nickname/display name, preferred sports (Badminton, Cricket, Football, Tennis, etc.), self-rated skill tier, age category, society/apartment unit details, and optional profile avatar.
  • Tournament Registrations: Event categories entered, doubles partner preferences, house/wing affiliation, and dietary or jersey preferences if requested by organizers.
  • Live Auction Participation: In-app bidding entries, team nominations, and squad allocation selections.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Device & Usage Data: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, IP address, app launch metrics, and performance crash logs collected via Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Local Persistence: Essential tournament cache stored locally on your device to ensure offline scorekeeping and fast bracket rendering.

3. Device Permissions Requested

ArenaOS requests the minimum set of permissions necessary to deliver sports tournament features:

Permission Platform Reason & Functional Purpose
Camera & Photos Android & iOS Allows you to upload your player profile picture, capture team logos, or scan tournament check-in QR codes. Completely optional.
Notifications Android & iOS Sends real-time court callout alerts (“Badminton Court 1 ready in 5 mins”), schedule delay notices, and live auction updates.
Internet & Network State Android & iOS Required for real-time Firestore database sync between players, umpires, and big-screen clubhouse scoreboards.

4. How We Use Your Information

We process your data strictly to provide and improve the ArenaOS tournament platform:

  • Draw & Fixture Generation: To seed players, calculate BYEs, balance doubles pairing pots, and prevent court schedule clashes.
  • Live Scoring & Scoreboards: To display real-time match scores, ball-by-ball updates, heat times, and broadcast results on venue TV displays.
  • Player Statistics: To calculate win-loss ratios, championship records, net run rates, and goal differentials across your sports career.
  • Communication & Alerts: To notify players of match timings, court assignments, and volunteer umpire assignments.
  • Platform Security & Fair Play: To detect fraudulent multiple entries or unauthorized score modifications via Volunteer PIN checks.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

We partner with industry-standard cloud and infrastructure providers who adhere to rigorous data privacy and security benchmarks:

  • Google Firebase: Used for secure authentication, real-time Firestore database storage, image hosting, and crash analytics. Firebase Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMob: May display non-intrusive rewarded advertisements or banners for free-tier users. Google Ads Privacy.
  • Google Play In-App Billing: Handles secure payment processing for Pro Tournament subscriptions. We never see or store your credit card or bank details.

6. Data Security & Retention

All data in transit between ArenaOS and our servers is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3). Data at rest is secured within Google Cloud data centers equipped with enterprise encryption (AES-256).

We retain tournament records and player career statistics so long as your account remains active. If an organization deletes a tournament or a user requests account removal, we permanently purge or anonymize the associated data in accordance with our retention policy.

7. Account & Data Deletion Policy

Google Play Data Safety & User Rights: You have the absolute right to request the permanent deletion of your ArenaOS user account and all associated personal data at any time, either directly from the mobile app or via web/email request without needing to open the app.

A. How to Delete Your Account and Data

We provide two simple methods to request permanent account deletion:

  1. In-App Deletion (Instant):
    Open ArenaOS → Tap on the Profile tab → Tap the Settings icon (&gear;) in the top-right → Select Account → Tap Delete Account & Data → Confirm your deletion. Your authentication tokens, personal profile, and private credentials will be immediately purged.
  2. Web / Email Deletion Request (Without App):
    If you have uninstalled the app or prefer web-based deletion, send an email to factictionary@gmail.com with the subject line ArenaOS Account and Data Deletion Request. Please include your registered email address or phone number. Our team will verify your request and permanently delete your account within 30 days. You will receive a confirmation email once complete.

B. Types of Data Deleted

Upon your deletion request, the following data will be permanently and irreversibly erased from our production databases (Firebase Auth, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Storage):

  • Personal Identifiers: Name, email address, phone number, and unique account ID (UID).
  • Player Profile & Media: Profile avatar photos, jersey preferences, player ratings, and apartment/society unit details.
  • Tournament Registrations: Direct registration entries, auction bids, and team nominations.

C. Data Retention & Anonymization

Match History & Public Records: Past completed matches, aggregate team scores, and historic tournament bracket results will be retained in an anonymized format to preserve fair-play records and historic standings for the community. Your personal name and profile picture will be replaced with “Former Player” and cannot be linked back to you.

Retention Timeline: All personal identifiers are permanently removed from live databases within 30 calendar days of receiving your request.

8. Children's Privacy

ArenaOS is intended for community sports tournaments and may include youth categories (e.g. Under-14 Badminton, Junior Cricket). Registrations for children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions) must be submitted by or with the verified consent of a parent, legal guardian, or authorized school/society coach.

We do not knowingly collect personal direct contact information from children without parental guidance.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect new platform capabilities or regulatory requirements. When material changes are made, we will notify you through an in-app banner or via email prior to the changes taking effect.
